Notifications


Data: FAA ATCSCC Airport Arrival Demand Chart (AADC) API, fly.faa.gov/aadc/api/airports, read live by your browser. Times are TFMS runway (wheels-off / wheels-on) times, not gate times. Airlines in FAA CDM (e.g. United and its regionals) get their EDCTs privately; for those the FAA publishes only the controlled arrival time (CTA). Airport time zones: embedded table (mwgg/Airports, IANA zones). Always confirm departure time with the airline / ATC.
